Calculator settings
Defaults: $1.50 customer shipping, $1.22 normal actual shipping, and $8.00 actual shipping for orders over $20. Buy offers are percentages of estimated TCGplayer net: $5 and under = 40% cash / 50% trade-in; $5–$20 = 50% cash / 60% trade-in; over $20 = 65% cash / 75% trade-in. Card-show selling prices round up to the configured whole-dollar increment. Buy offers use $0.25 increments under $5, $0.50 increments from $5–$20, and $1 increments over $20. Cash and trade-in profit percentages are calculated as profit ÷ purchase price (markup on your buy cost).
